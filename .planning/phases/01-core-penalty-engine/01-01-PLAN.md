**As an** organizer, **I want to** start an Americano tournament and have all rounds pre-calculated with level-aware, penalty-scored matchups, **so that** every player gets fair pairings from round 1 through the final round without manual intervention.

<objective>

Purpose: Replaces the naive round-builder (which ignores player levels and has no rotation fairness) with a greedy penalty-scored algorithm that produces a full pre-calculated schedule before the tournament starts. The schedule enforces skill-level balance, partner rotation, court rotation, and rest fairness — constrained by configurable penalty weights and threshold-based relaxation.
